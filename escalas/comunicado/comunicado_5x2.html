Coordinators & Supervisors

Trios that
take turns

Same 21-day cycle, one fixed shift, no separate training day.
Every day 2 of 3 are on shift; on Saturday Overlap, all 3.

Operational Coordinators
One per unit and shift: 3 per shift. The other two cover the unit of whoever is off.
Operational Supervisors
One per core: 3 per unit and shift. Each OS follows the days off of their own core.
